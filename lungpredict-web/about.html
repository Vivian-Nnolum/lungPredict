About LungPredict

LungPredict is an advanced early detection screening tool designed to empower individuals with instant, data-backed insights regarding their lung health. Recognizing that early identification of lung conditions is critical for positive therapeutic outcomes, our system provides quick, non-invasive risk assessments to facilitate timely discussions with healthcare professionals.

The application utilizes statistical modeling and machine learning algorithms (powered by our FastAPI backend) that process diverse factors, including patient demographic characteristics, lifestyle factors, hereditary and environmental respiratory histories, and specific physiological symptoms.

Please Note: LungPredict is designed to serve as an educational and screening companion tool. It is not a substitute for professional clinical advice, official physical examination, diagnostic imaging (like chest CT scans), or a medical doctor's diagnosis.

Key Features

ML Powered

Uses trained machine learning classification algorithms to evaluate clinical characteristics and map out predictive hazard profiles.

Early Detection

Helps in identifying potential warning signs and elevated risks early, laying the foundation for timely proactive medical intervention.

Risk Assessment

Provides a clear risk classification level (Low, Medium, High) accompanied by an actionable percentile-probability estimation.

Secure & Private

Your entered physiological features and lifestyle assessments are processed entirely securely and handled with strict privacy.
